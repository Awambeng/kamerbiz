# Project overview

## What is KamerBiz?

KamerBiz is a proposed digital platform for small businesses in Cameroon. It will help business owners show their products online, receive and organise orders, follow payments and understand how their business is doing.

The aim is to make useful business tools easier to access, including for owners who currently manage much of their work through phone calls, WhatsApp, social media and notebooks.

## Why are we building it?

Many small businesses work hard but do not have simple tools to keep their products, orders, customers and sales information organised. This can lead to missed orders, slow replies, mistakes and limited information for making decisions.

KamerBiz aims to reduce these problems with one simple platform.

These problems are starting points for research. We must speak with business owners to learn which problems happen most often and which ones they would pay to solve.

## Main product areas

1. **Sell:** Give each business an online store and a clear way for customers to place orders.
2. **Manage:** Help the owner manage products, orders, payments and delivery.
3. **Grow:** Show useful information about sales, products and customers.

These are product areas, not separate applications.

## Who is it for first?

The first version will focus on businesses that sell physical products, such as fashion, perfume, cosmetics and other everyday goods.

The platform should be able to support other types of business later. The first release will not try to serve every type of business.

## What does success mean?

KamerBiz should help a business owner:

- Spend less time organising orders.
- Miss fewer customer requests.
- Know which orders have been paid for and completed.
- Keep useful customer and product records.
- Understand which products and channels bring sales.

We will measure these results during a small pilot. We will not assume that software alone guarantees more sales.
