# KamerBiz project charter

## 1. Purpose

Build a simple platform that helps small businesses in Cameroon sell online, manage daily work and make better business decisions.

## 2. Vision

A Cameroon where small businesses have access to useful tools and opportunities that help them thrive.

## 3. Mission

Make it easier for Cameroonian small businesses to serve customers and run their businesses with simple, accessible technology.

## 4. Brand direction

- **Working name:** KamerBiz
- **Main feeling:** Hope
- **Working message:** Stronger businesses. A stronger Cameroon.
- **Brand promise:** Practical tools that make running a business easier.

The name and message are working choices. Name, domain and trademark checks are still required.

## 5. First users

Small businesses that sell physical products. Early groups to learn from include fashion sellers, perfume sellers, cosmetics sellers and general product retailers.

## 6. Main goals

- Give each business a simple online storefront.
- Make it easy for customers to place an order.
- Give owners one place to manage orders and payment checks.
- Help owners keep product and customer information.
- Provide simple reports that support business decisions.

## 7. First release boundaries

The first release should focus on:
- Business registration and store setup.
- Product and category management.
- Public storefront.
- Cart and order submission.
- Order management.
- Manual payment evidence review.
- Basic order tracking.
- Basic business reports.
- English and French interface support.

The first release should not include a full accounting system, a complex marketing system, a delivery fleet, or automatic payment confirmation unless research shows a clear need and the team agrees to expand the scope.

## 8. How we will work

1. Learn from business owners.
2. Write down the problem and evidence.
3. Agree on a small solution.
4. Design and test it with users.
5. Build in small steps.
6. Test with real businesses.
7. Improve based on feedback.

## 9. Success measures

During the pilot, track:
- Number of orders recorded in KamerBiz.
- Time needed to process an order.
- Number of orders with confirmed payment.
- Number of completed orders.
- Repeat customer activity.
- Feedback from business owners and customers.

## 10. Current status

This charter records the current direction. Product details, pricing, technical choices and launch dates may change after research.
