# Key workflows

## 1. Business setup

1. Owner creates an account.
2. Owner enters business details.
3. Owner chooses a store name and link.
4. Owner adds contact, delivery and payment information.
5. Owner adds products.
6. Owner previews the store.
7. Owner publishes the store.

## 2. Customer places an order

1. Customer opens the store.
2. Customer views products.
3. Customer adds products to the cart.
4. Customer enters name, phone number and delivery or pickup details.
5. Customer reviews the order.
6. Customer submits the order.
7. Platform creates an order reference and tracking link.
8. Business receives the new order.

The business may either accept the order directly or confirm product availability before asking the customer to pay. This choice should be checked during user research.

## 3. Payment review

1. Customer follows the business's payment instructions.
2. Customer submits a transaction reference and screenshot when requested.
3. Business opens the payment review.
4. Business checks its actual MoMo or Orange Money account.
5. Business confirms or rejects the payment.
6. Platform records who made the decision and when.

A screenshot is not proof that money arrived.

## 4. Order fulfilment

1. Business accepts the order.
2. Business prepares the items.
3. Business marks the order ready for pickup or dispatch.
4. Business records delivery progress.
5. Business marks the order delivered or completed.
6. Customer can view the latest status.

## 5. Customer tracking

1. Customer opens the private tracking link.
2. Platform checks the link.
3. Customer sees only the information needed to follow that order.
4. Customer does not see private business information or other customers' orders.

## 6. Separate order, payment and delivery status

These statuses describe different things.

| Area     | Example statuses                                                         |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Order    | New, accepted, processing, completed, cancelled                          |
| Payment  | Unpaid, evidence submitted, checking, paid, rejected, refunded           |
| Delivery | Not arranged, preparing, ready for pickup, dispatched, delivered, failed |

For example, an order can be accepted while its payment is still being checked and delivery has not started.
