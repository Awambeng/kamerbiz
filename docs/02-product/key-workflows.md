# Key workflows

## 1. Business setup

1. Owner creates a business account.
2. Owner enters business details and configures a public store.
3. Owner adds contact, delivery and payment information.
4. Owner adds products, prices and stock.
5. Owner previews and publishes the store.

## 2. Enable a product for agent promotion

1. Vendor opens a product in the business dashboard.
2. Vendor enables the product for the KamerBiz agent network.
3. Vendor sets the commission amount or rate and any clear promotion conditions.
4. The product becomes discoverable to agents.
5. Vendor can pause or disable future promotion at any time.
6. Existing attributed orders and commission records remain intact.

A vendor does not approve individual agents before they can register or promote eligible products.

## 3. Agent joins and discovers products

1. Any person creates an agent account through self-service registration.
2. No vendor invitation, vendor approval or manual identity validation is required.
3. Agent opens the agent area and browses eligible products.
4. Agent can discover products from multiple businesses in one account.
5. Agent reviews the vendor's product information, price, commission and promotion conditions.
6. Agent generates or copies a unique product tracking link.
7. Agent shares the link and product information through social media, messaging apps or other channels.

## 4. Customer buys through an agent link

1. Customer opens the agent's product link.
2. KamerBiz records the referral token and the applicable attribution context.
3. Customer views the vendor's product page and can continue browsing the store.
4. Customer checks out as a guest and submits the order.
5. The order item stores the attributed agent and a snapshot of the applicable commission terms.
6. Vendor receives and reviews the order.
7. Vendor confirms availability and updates the order status.
8. Customer pays the vendor directly.
9. Vendor checks the actual payment account and confirms or rejects payment.
10. Vendor fulfils the order.
11. When the item meets commission eligibility rules, the commission becomes payable.

The customer experience remains a purchase from the business. The agent referral should not make the checkout confusing or require the customer to create an agent account.

## 5. Commission lifecycle

1. Referral is attributed to an order item.
2. Commission is recorded as pending while the order and payment are unresolved.
3. If the item is paid and successfully fulfilled, commission becomes payable.
4. If the item is cancelled, unpaid, rejected, refunded or returned, the commission is cancelled or reversed according to the recorded event.
5. Vendor pays the agent outside KamerBiz in the initial release.
6. Vendor records the payout amount, date and reference or note.
7. Agent sees the updated paid status and payout record.

Commission must be calculated per order item because a single order can contain products with different vendors, agents or commission terms.

## 6. Direct customer order

1. Customer opens a business store without an agent referral.
2. Customer submits an order as a guest.
3. Vendor reviews and fulfils the order.
4. The order is recorded as direct unless a valid referral attribution applies under the platform's attribution rules.

## 7. Payment review

1. Customer follows the business's payment instructions.
2. Customer submits a transaction reference and screenshot when requested.
3. Business checks its actual MoMo or Orange Money account.
4. Business confirms or rejects the payment.
5. Platform records who made the decision and when.

A screenshot is not proof that money arrived.

## 8. Order fulfilment

1. Business accepts the order.
2. Business prepares the items.
3. Business marks the order ready for pickup or dispatch.
4. Business records delivery progress.
5. Business marks the order delivered or completed.
6. Customer can view the latest status.

## 9. Separate lifecycle statuses

| Area       | Example statuses                                                         |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Order      | New, accepted, processing, completed, cancelled                          |
| Payment    | Unpaid, evidence submitted, checking, paid, rejected, refunded           |
| Fulfilment | Not arranged, preparing, ready for pickup, dispatched, delivered, failed |
| Commission | Pending, payable, paid, reversed, disputed                               |

An order can be accepted while payment is still being checked and fulfilment has not started.
