# Users and needs

## Main user groups

### Business owner

The owner sets up the store, adds products, receives orders and manages the business.

#### Needs

- Set up a store without technical help.
- Add and update products easily.
- See new orders quickly.
- Check payment evidence.
- Know which orders need attention.
- Understand basic sales activity.

### Customer

The customer visits a store, checks products and places an order.

#### Needs

- Understand what the business sells.
- See clear prices and product details.
- Place an order without being forced to create an account.
- Know how to pay.
- Know what happens after placing an order.
- Contact the business when needed.

### Platform administrator

The administrator supports the platform and manages business accounts.

#### Needs

- Review business registrations.
- Help with account and store problems.
- Manage access when required.
- See platform activity needed for support.
- Keep a record of important administrative actions.

## Problems to validate

These are research questions, not confirmed facts:

- Do owners lose orders when messages arrive through different channels?
- How do owners currently check stock?
- How do they confirm MoMo or Orange Money payments?
- How do they organise delivery and pickup?
- How often do customers ask for order updates?
- What information do owners use to decide which products to restock?
- Would owners pay for a tool that solves these problems?

## Research rule

Do not build a feature only because it sounds useful. First understand who has the problem, how often it happens, what they do today and how much the problem costs them in time or money.
