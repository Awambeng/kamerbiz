# First release scope

MVP means the first small version of the product that real businesses can use and test.

## Included in the first release

### Business account and store

- Business owner registration and login.
- Basic business profile.
- Store name and public store link.
- Store contact and delivery information.

### Products

- Add, edit and remove products.
- Product name, description, price and images.
- Product categories.
- Basic stock quantity.

### Customer store

- View products and categories.
- View product details.
- Add products to a cart.
- Submit an order as a guest.
- Receive an order reference and private tracking link.

### Orders

- View and search orders.
- Accept or reject an order.
- Update order progress.
- Record cancellation.

### Payments

- Business can add payment instructions and payment numbers.
- Customer can submit a transaction reference and payment screenshot when required.
- Business can confirm or reject the payment after checking its own account.

### Delivery and pickup

- Business can set basic delivery information and fees.
- Business can offer pickup.
- Business can update fulfilment progress.

### Basic reports

- Orders placed.
- Payments confirmed.
- Orders completed.
- Simple product performance.
- Order source when a source can be recorded.

### Languages

- English and French interface.

## Not included at first

- Platform holding customer funds.
- Automatic payment confirmation.
- Full accounting and tax management.
- Delivery driver marketplace.
- Complex customer loyalty programme.
- Advanced advertising tools.
- Native mobile applications.
- Many staff roles and approval levels.
- Support for every business type from day one.

## Important rules

- A screenshot alone must never mark a payment as paid.
- An order, its payment and its delivery progress must be tracked separately.
- A customer should not need an account to place an order.
- A business must only access its own private data.
- Stock changes must be handled safely to reduce the risk of selling more items than are available.

## How scope changes

A new feature must have a clear user problem, a reason to be included now and an agreed test for success. Otherwise, record it for later.
