# Project roadmap

The roadmap shows the order of work. Dates will be added after the team understands the research, effort and available time.

## Stage 1: Learn from businesses

**Goal:** Understand how target businesses currently sell and manage orders.

Work:

- Speak with fashion, perfume, cosmetics and general product sellers.
- Observe how they receive orders.
- Learn how they check stock and payments.
- Understand delivery and pickup.
- Ask what they would pay to solve.
- Record evidence and open questions.

**Ready to move on when:** The team can explain the main problem using real examples from business owners.

## Stage 2: Confirm the product

**Goal:** Agree on the first users, main problem and first release.

Work:

- Review research.
- Choose the first user group.
- Confirm the most important workflows.
- Agree on what is included and excluded.
- Write clear requirements.

**Ready to move on when:** The first release has a clear and manageable scope.

## Stage 3: Design and test

**Goal:** Make the product easy to understand before building all of it.

Work:

- Create the design system.
- Design the vendor dashboard.
- Design the customer store and checkout.
- Test the screens with potential users.
- Improve confusing steps.

**Ready to move on when:** Users can complete the main tasks in a design test.

## Stage 4: Build the first release

**Goal:** Build and test the agreed features.

Suggested order:

1. Accounts and business setup.
2. Products and categories.
3. Public storefront.
4. Cart and checkout.
5. Orders and tracking.
6. Payment evidence review.
7. Delivery and pickup.
8. Basic reports.
9. Platform administration.

## Stage 5: Pilot

**Goal:** Test KamerBiz with a small group of real businesses.

Work:

- Onboard a small number of businesses.
- Help them add products.
- Observe real orders.
- Collect feedback from owners and customers.
- Measure time saved and order handling.
- Fix the biggest problems.

## Stage 6: Improve and prepare launch

**Goal:** Improve reliability and confirm that the product offers enough value.

Work:

- Fix pilot issues.
- Review support needs.
- Confirm pricing through customer feedback.
- Prepare onboarding material.
- Check security, backups and support processes.
- Decide whether the product is ready for a wider launch.

## Roadmap rule

Do not start a later stage just because the calendar says so. Move forward when the current stage has produced the evidence and work needed for the next one.
