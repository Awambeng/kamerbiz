# Market research plan

## Purpose

Learn how small businesses in Cameroon work today and which problems are important enough to solve.

Do not use research only to confirm ideas the team already likes. Be ready to change the product if business owners show that another problem matters more.

## Who to speak with

Start with owners of:

- Fashion businesses.
- Perfume businesses.
- Cosmetics businesses.
- General product shops.

Try to include businesses of different sizes and levels of digital experience.

## Interview questions

1. How do customers usually find your business?
2. How do customers place orders?
3. Where do you receive customer messages?
4. How do you record each order?
5. How do you know what is in stock?
6. How do customers usually pay?
7. How do you confirm that a payment arrived?
8. How do you arrange delivery or pickup?
9. What part of this process takes the most time?
10. Tell me about the last time an order was missed or delayed.
11. What tools do you currently use?
12. Have you paid for a tool to help with your business? What happened?
13. If you could improve one part of the process, what would it be?

Ask for real examples. Avoid asking only whether someone likes the idea of KamerBiz.

## What to record

For each interview, record:

- Date.
- Business type.
- General business size.
- Current process.
- Problems described.
- How often each problem happens.
- Current workaround.
- Time or money lost, if known.
- Tools currently used.
- Interest in testing a solution.
- Follow-up questions.

Do not record private customer information unless it is necessary and the person has agreed.

## How to review the results

Group similar problems. Look for problems that:

- Happen often.
- Cause clear loss of time or money.
- Affect several businesses.
- Are not already solved well by the tools owners use.
- Have a solution owners are willing to try or pay for.

## Research output

Create a short summary with:

- What we heard.
- What evidence supports it.
- What surprised us.
- Which problems matter most.
- What we should change in the product.
- What we still do not know.
