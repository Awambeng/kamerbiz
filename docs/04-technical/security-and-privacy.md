# Security and privacy basics

## Information we may store

- Business account details.
- Business products and images.
- Customer names and phone numbers.
- Order details and delivery information.
- Payment transaction references and submitted evidence.
- Order and payment history.

Collect only information needed to provide the service.

## Who can see the information?

- A business owner can see information belonging to their business.
- A customer can see the limited details for their own order through a private tracking link.
- Platform support staff can access information only when needed for support and according to clear access rules.
- One business must never see another business's private information.

## Important protections

- Check permissions on the backend for every private request.
- Keep uploaded payment evidence private.
- Use secure links that are difficult to guess.
- Do not show full customer details on public pages.
- Record important changes to orders and payments.
- Protect account login details.
- Use secure connections.
- Set rules for how long records and uploaded files are kept.

## Payment safety

KamerBiz will not ask customers to share their MoMo or Orange Money PINs.

A payment screenshot can be changed or reused. The business must check its own payment account before confirming a payment.

## Before launch

The team must review applicable privacy and data protection requirements in Cameroon and any other country where the service operates. This document is a starting checklist, not legal advice.
