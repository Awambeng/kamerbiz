# KamerBiz brand direction

## Brand name

**KamerBiz**

This is the selected working name. Domain, social handle and trademark availability still need to be checked.

## Main feeling

**Hope**

KamerBiz should help business owners feel that progress is possible and that they have practical support as they build their businesses.

## Purpose

Help Cameroonian businesses become more organised, serve customers better and grow with useful technology.

## Brand character

- Hopeful, but honest.
- Helpful, not complicated.
- Modern, but welcoming.
- Confident, but not loud.
- Proud of Cameroon, without relying on stereotypes.

## Working messages

Main message:

**Stronger businesses. A stronger Cameroon.**

Supporting message:

**Simple tools to sell, manage and grow.**

These are draft messages. Test them with business owners before treating them as final.

## Visual direction

| Colour        | Hex value | Intended meaning            |
| ------------- | --------- | --------------------------- |
| Forest green  | `#173D32` | Trust, growth and stability |
| Golden yellow | `#E5B94F` | Hope and opportunity        |
| Warm ivory    | `#F7F5EF` | Clarity and warmth          |
| Coral         | `#E4775C` | Energy and friendliness     |

Use the colours with care. Pages must remain easy to read and accessible.

## Design principles

- Design for mobile phones first.
- Make common tasks easy to find.
- Use clear words and helpful instructions.
- Keep forms short.
- Show clear feedback after an action.
- Use animation only when it helps explain an action.
- Keep pages fast and usable on weaker connections.
- Support English and French across the product.

## What to avoid

- Too many colours on one screen.
- Busy layouts and unnecessary decoration.
- Technical words that customers do not understand.
- Images that use African culture as a visual stereotype.
- Promises that KamerBiz can guarantee business growth.
